A business with a team of photographers and content creators who do photo, video, and graphic design for hire.

Target Audience

The target audience of this website would be anybody who needs content for their needs. High Schools who need portraits for sports teams, or games to be covered. Or even other businesses who need staff portaits. News Outlets who need pictures, video, or graphics. People who need weddings or events covered would use this website to hire photographers or videographers, etc...

Their primary task and goals would to be getting their events or needs covered by a camera through photo, video, and graphic design, whatever they need.
